Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions explain how Prime Pops handles enquiries, orders, bulk orders and deliveries.

Orders & Confirmation

Orders and enquiries are handled through WhatsApp or other communication channels provided by Prime Pops.

An order is considered confirmed only after Prime Pops has responded and the relevant order details, pricing, availability and delivery arrangements have been agreed.

Pricing

Prices may change from time to time. The price applicable to an order will be communicated during the order confirmation process.

Bulk Orders

For larger, custom or event orders, Prime Pops may require an advance deposit before preparation begins. Any required deposit and payment arrangements will be communicated during order confirmation.

Bulk orders should normally be discussed at least 2–3 days in advance to allow sufficient preparation time.

Changes & Cancellations

Please contact Prime Pops as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order.

Changes or cancellations may not be possible once preparation has started, particularly for custom or bulk orders.

Delivery

Prime Pops is a delivery-only brand.

Delivery availability and fees depend on the customer's location. Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by traffic, weather, location or other circumstances outside our control.

Product Availability

Flavours and products are subject to availability. Prime Pops may occasionally be unable to fulfil a requested product or flavour and will communicate this where applicable.

Order Refusal or Cancellation

Prime Pops reserves the right to decline or cancel an order where necessary, including where a requested product is unavailable, an order cannot reasonably be fulfilled, or agreed payment or delivery arrangements are not completed.
