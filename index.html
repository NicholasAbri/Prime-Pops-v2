FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

Here are a few things you may want to know before reaching out to Prime Pops.

How do I place an order?

Simply contact Prime Pops through WhatsApp and let us know what you'd like. We'll guide you through the available options and arrange delivery.

When is my popcorn prepared?

Our popcorn is prepared fresh for your order to help maintain its quality and freshness.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Prime Pops is a delivery-only brand. Delivery availability and fees depend on your location.

Do you cater for birthdays, church programs, schools, offices, and events?

Yes. We accept enquiries for events and bulk orders, including birthdays, church activities, school events, office gatherings, weddings, parties, and other special occasions.

What flavours are available?

We currently offer Milky and Caramel popcorn. More flavours may be added over time.

What kinds of occasions do you serve?

Prime Pops serves individual customers as well as offices, parties, weddings, church activities, school events, meetings, and other gatherings.

Is there a walk-in location?

No. Prime Pops is currently delivery-only, so orders are arranged remotely and delivered to you.
