Privacy Policy

Prime Pops respects your privacy and aims to keep customer information limited to what is reasonably needed to communicate with customers, arrange orders and provide delivery services.

Information We May Receive

When you contact Prime Pops or make an enquiry or order, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery address or location
  • Your order or enquiry details
  • Other information you choose to provide to us

How We Use Your Information

We may use this information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Discuss and confirm orders
  • Arrange payment and delivery
  • Communicate with you through WhatsApp or other agreed channels
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our products and customer service

WhatsApp and Other Service Providers

Customer communications may take place through WhatsApp, which is a third-party communication service.

Where delivery or other services are provided by another party, relevant information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to complete the requested service.

Data Sharing

Prime Pops does not sell customer information.

We do not share customer information for unrelated purposes. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to fulfil an order, arrange delivery, provide a requested service, comply with a legal obligation, or protect the business and its customers.

Data Retention

We aim to keep customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or where we are required to retain it.

Security

Prime Pops takes reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Privacy

If you have a question about how Prime Pops handles your information, or you would like to raise a privacy concern, please contact us through WhatsApp.
